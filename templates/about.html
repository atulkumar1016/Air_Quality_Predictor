About AQI Shield 🌍

A smart air quality analysis platform that helps people understand and respond to pollution levels in real time.

🎯 What is AQI Shield?

AQI Shield ek smart web application hai jo air pollution ko analyze aur predict karta hai. Yeh system PM2.5, PM10, NO₂ aur SO₂ jaise pollutants ka use karke AQI calculate karta hai aur logon ko real-time health advisory deta hai.

"Is project ka goal hai logon ko real-time awareness dena taaki wo apni health protect kar saken."

⚙️ Technologies Used

Built using a modern Python-based stack with a clean HTML/CSS frontend:

Python (Flask)
Data Analysis (Pandas)
Matplotlib / Seaborn
Frontend (HTML, CSS)

🌱 Our Mission

Air pollution is one of the leading environmental health risks globally. AQI Shield aims to make air quality data accessible and understandable for everyone — from students and researchers to everyday citizens who want to know if it's safe to step outside.
