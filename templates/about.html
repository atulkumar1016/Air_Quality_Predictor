About AQI Shield

AQI Shield is India's most advanced real-time Air Quality Intelligence platform — built to protect the health of 1.4 billion people.

Our mission is simple: Make every Indian breathe safer.

🌍 Our Vision

To create a future where every citizen has access to clean, breathable air and real-time awareness about environmental conditions that affect their health.

🚀 What AQI Shield Offers

  • ⚡ Real-time Air Quality Monitoring across India
  • 🤖 AI-based AQI Prediction for next 24 hours
  • 📡 Hyperlocal Pollution Tracking (street-level accuracy)
  • 📊 Open Datasets for researchers & students
  • 📱 Health Alerts & exposure guidance
  • 🌫️ PM2.5 / PM10 / NO₂ / SO₂ / CO / O₃ pollutant breakdown

❤️ Why We Built This

Every year millions of Indians suffer from the effects of air pollution.
AQI Shield aims to bridge the gap between raw environmental data and meaningful health insights — so you can make smarter choices about your day.
