About this project

About AQI Shield 🌍

A smart air quality prediction platform helping people understand and respond to pollution levels across Indian cities.

🎯 What is AQI Shield?

AQI Shield ek smart web application hai jo air pollution ko analyze aur predict karta hai. Yeh system PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂ aur SO₂ jaise pollutants ka use karke ek trained ML model ke through AQI calculate karta hai — aur logon ko real-time health advisory deta hai.

"Is project ka goal hai logon ko real-time awareness dena taaki wo apni health protect kar saken."

🤖 ML Model Details

Prediction ek trained Scikit-learn model se hoti hai jo cleaned_pollution_data.csv pe train kiya gaya hai. Model 4 features leta hai — PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂, SO₂ — aur AQI predict karta hai.

🌱 Our Mission

Air pollution is one of the leading environmental health risks globally. AQI Shield aims to make air quality data accessible and understandable for everyone — from students and researchers to everyday citizens who want to know if it's safe to step outside.
